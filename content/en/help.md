# Where to start

In one line: this page does one thing. It sends you to the right page for where you are now.

## Four ways in, pick one

| Where you are | Start here | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Just heard about it, want to know if it concerns me | [For non-technical readers](/en/why/) | 4 minutes to read |
| I install nothing. I only want to know whether to have IT install it | [Five questions your customer will ask](/en/answers/), [the paragraph on the front page](/en/#forward) | 2 minutes to read |
| Ready to install, or installed and unsure what comes next | [Install](/en/docs/install/), [Five-minute start](/en/docs/quickstart/) | 5 minutes to install |
| Installed, and something is off | the five items under "Stuck" below | 2 minutes |

## Stuck: the five most common cases

1. **A command does nothing.** Check the package first: `antinel --version`. Whether the install worked is in the last section of [install](/en/docs/install/).
2. **There are no records at all.** The checkpoint never ran. See the first section of [troubleshooting](/en/docs/troubleshooting/).
3. **Too many notices.** A critical block cannot be relaxed. The rest can take an allowlist. See [policy and allowlists](/en/docs/policy/).
4. **You want to know what it actually stopped.** Read [the six verdicts](/en/verdicts/) first, then [the checkup](/en/checkup/).
5. **You must show this to someone non-technical.** [The sample report](/en/sample/) is that sheet.

## You want to check, not to take our word

- Where a number comes from: [facts](/en/facts/) gives the source for each anchor. The raw values are in [the datasets](/en/datasets/).
- Whether someone altered a record: [audit chain and re-runs](/en/audit/) gives the commands to re-run.
- What we will and will not do: [license and neutrality](/en/license/), [threat model and limits](/en/threat-model/).

## When a word does not make sense

A term gets one explanation the first time it appears. For the settled wording see [brand and words](/en/brand/). The meaning of each of the six verdicts is in [the six verdicts](/en/verdicts/).

## What this page does not do

- It does not decide for you whether to use this. The answer depends on whether you are missing this book of records.
- It does not repeat the body of another page. It gives signposts only.
