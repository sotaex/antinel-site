# Five questions your customer will ask

One line: this page does not sell anything. It is a card of answers — read it when the question comes.

## Five questions, five answers you can give on the spot

| Your customer asks | You answer | What holds the sentence up |
|---|---|---|
| We use AI tools. Can our data leak out? | We ran a local check. Every tool gets one result | One page |
| How do we know you wrote it yourself? | Records are append-only. Change one byte and it shows | `antinel verify` |
| If something goes wrong, how fast do you find it? | A dangerous action stops on the spot. What we cannot stop still leaves a record | {{facts.F-001.value.rules}} rules |
| When was the last check, and where is the result? | Here is the last one, with a date | A dated report |
| Who owns this inside your company? | Name your technical lead and how to reach them | You fill this in |

## Why this card works

None of the five answers comes from an adjective. All five need the same three things: one check, one page, one date.

You do not need to read code. You need to know how to say these five lines, and who can hand you that page in two minutes.

## What this card cannot do

- It cannot settle the question for your customer. That customer reads the record, or does not.
- It is not legal advice. For legal force, go to an accredited testing body.
- "Not checked" never turns into "nothing found". Those two are not the same sentence.
