# Free and paid

One line: the software is free and the service is paid. This page says which is which, and leaves no room to guess.

## One table

| | Personal | Institution |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free forever | First year free, then half price |
| Machines | Your own machine | Every machine you name |
| Results you see | All of them | All of them |
| Who looks | You | Team, customers, board |
| Records kept | On this machine | Here, plus a company summary |
| Issued under a name | Only for yourself | Yes, under your name |
| Support | Community | Email, answered in 24 hours |
| Paid services | None | Deployment, training, integration |

**The third row matters most.** The personal edition and the institution edition show the same results. Not one is hidden.

Why we split it this way: we do not earn by hiding things. Let the personal edition hide a result, and "not checked is not the same as no record found" becomes a lie.

What you pay for is not features. It is two things: many machines in one book, and a name on the paper.

## First institutions

Five places. The list closes when it fills. Five places means five places.

The first year costs nothing. After that, half of the public price, permanently.

The half price follows the public price of the day. Raise the public price and your fifty percent stays.

The half price is thanks for joining early. This is no promotion.

## Three promises

What we ship today is version 0.x. Our promises sit in public files, not in speech.

1. **An upgrade keeps your records.** The record format stays backward compatible. A record from today still reads and still verifies after next year's upgrade.
2. **We announce a shutdown first.** Any shutdown comes with 12 months notice and an offline export.
3. **Free stays free.** What costs nothing now will not start costing money because of an upgrade.

## Limits of the half price

- The half price does not transfer. It stays with the entity that first deployed.
- It covers the machines on the first list only. New machines pay the public price.
- On a merger, a split, or a change of control, both sides confirm in writing whether the discount continues.

## What we do not do

No "certification". No "passing fee". No "help me pass the check". No paid ranking, no paid placement.

The reason is practical. Once money can change a conclusion, the record is worth nothing to anyone, including the person who paid.
