# Who runs this

One line: four questions, four answers — who runs it, how it earns, who to write to, and what happens if we stop.

## Who runs it

**Beijing Zhilv Technology Co., Ltd.**

Antinel Steward is a product of this company. We publish the code under Apache-2.0. The repository is public, and you can read the commit history.

The company's view: AI tools will keep reading files and running commands on personal machines. Nothing records that today.

So we keep the record.

## How it earns

The software is free. The service is paid. This is not charity, and it is not a trap.

- **Personal**: free forever on your own machine. We cut no feature. You see the whole truth, but only for this one machine.
- **Institution**: the first year costs nothing. After that, it costs half the public price, for good. That half price thanks the early adopters. It is not a promotion.
- **Paid services**: deployment, training, integration with your own systems, and a summary we issue under your name.

What we do not sell: no "certification", no "passing fee", no "help me pass the check" work.

Judgment and payment stay apart. That is the only thing here worth money. A conclusion should not please the person who pays.

## Who to write to

**contact@antinel.com. We answer within 24 hours.**

Put `SECURITY` in the subject line for a security report. Those take a separate path.

This mailbox is not a ticket system. Mail reaches a person.

Add the tool name, your system version, and the result you saw. That saves one round trip.

## If we stop

Our code stays open under Apache-2.0. The rules and the records live on your machine.

Even if this project stops updating tomorrow, you can still check your own log. `antinel verify` needs no server of ours, and no network.

In other markets "open source" means "free". Here it means this: if the vendor disappears, your check still works.

## What we do not claim to be

- Not a certification body. We issue no certificates, and we sell none.
- Not a testing laboratory. Ours is not an accredited test report, and it carries no legal force.
- Not a third-party audit. An audit needs a qualification and a mandate. This is a recorder you install yourself.
